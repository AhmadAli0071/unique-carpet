Why wool

Wool is still the better fibre

Wool is a natural wonder: it gives a carpet durability, resilience, natural stain resistance and eco-friendly credentials that no synthetic quite matches. It springs back under furniture, it traps dust and allergens out of the air, and it quietens a room.

We stock synthetics too, and we will tell you honestly when they are the smarter buy.
